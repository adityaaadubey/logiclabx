Gate Properties & Hardware Usage
An AND gate implements logical conjunction. In physical IC form (such as the standard 7408 TTL chip), it comprises multiple internal NPN/NMOS transistors configured such that output Y is pulled HIGH only when both input signals are at logic 1 (+5V).
