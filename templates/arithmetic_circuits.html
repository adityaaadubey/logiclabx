Practical Applications in Modern Hardware
In modern microprocessors, Half Adders and Full Adders form the bedrock of the Arithmetic Logic Unit (ALU). Multiple 1-bit full adders are cascaded to form 32-bit and 64-bit Ripple Carry Adders (RCA), Carry-Lookahead Adders (CLA), and digital multipliers that execute billions of calculations per second.
